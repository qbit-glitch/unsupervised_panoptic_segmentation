## CAUSE-TR DINOv3 v6 — HP Backbone Fine-tuning (Stage 0 Launched)

### What Changed vs v5

v5 bugs:
- Backbone was **FROZEN** — only trained TRDecoder head (28M params instead of full 303M)
- `tau=0.07` (wrong — official Cityscapes config uses 0.6)
- `rho=0.7` (wrong — official uses 0.02, batch-scaled to 0.32 for bs=4)
- 5 epochs (wrong — official 30 epochs)
- Missing consistency loss, missing `project_head`, Pool_sp renewed per epoch instead of every 20 iters

v6 fixes all of these exactly per `refs/hp/json/server/cityscapes.json`.

### Implementation Decisions

1. **VQ collapse (diagnosed and fixed)**: `segment.head` (TRDecoder with VQ) collapses all features to the same codebook entry when backbone features shift away from the v4 codebook distribution. `cons=0.0000` exactly, loss frozen at 63.232577 to 6 decimal places. **Fix**: replaced `segment.head` with temporary `nn.Linear(DIM, REDUCED_DIM)` code_head that bypasses VQ entirely — full gradient flow through backbone.

2. **rho batch-scaling**: Official `rho=0.02` designed for bs=64 → 512 negatives/anchor. At bs=4, only 32 negatives/anchor → too noisy. **Fix**: `HP_RHO = 0.32` (512 negs ÷ 4×400 patches = 0.32).

3. **Gradient accumulation (bs=64 effective)**: Native bs=64 through ViT-L/16 on MPS → OOM (6.7 GB/layer). **Fix**: `--accum_steps 16`, physical bs=4 × 16 = effective bs=64. `lmbd` warmup tied to optimizer steps, not physical iterations.

4. **code_head_ema**: `copy.deepcopy(code_head)` used as EMA teacher for Pool_sp renewal and `modeloutput_z_mix` computation. `HP_EMA_DECAY=0.999`.
