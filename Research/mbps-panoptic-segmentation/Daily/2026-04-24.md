# Daily — 2026-04-24

## Meeting with NeurIPS Professor (Advisor Discussion)

**Topic:** Where LoRA/DoRA adapters belong in the MBPS pipeline.

### Key Advice
> "Do NOT use LoRA/DoRA in Stage-2 and Stage-3. You are already doing full backpropagation there — adding DoRA doesn't make sense. Use DoRA only in the pseudo-label generation frozen models: CAUSE-TR and the Depth Estimation models (DepthPro, DA3, DA2-Large, etc.)."

### Why This Makes Sense
| Stage | Backprop | Adapter Role | Verdict |
|-------|----------|--------------|---------|
| Stage 1 (pseudo-label generation) | Frozen pretrained models | **DoRA** → domain adaptation without GT labels | ✅ Correct |
| Stage 2 (M2F training) | Full backprop | DoRA redundant — full model already adapts | ❌ Remove |
| Stage 3 (self-training / CUPS) | Full backprop + EMA teacher | DoRA corrupts features, weakens EMA teacher | ❌ Remove |

### Empirical Confirmation from Our Own Results
The `analysis_docs/why_conv_dora_not_plain_lora.md` already proved this empirically:

| Backbone | Stage-2 PQ | Stage-3 PQ | Stage-3 Gain |
|----------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| Frozen | 28.09 | **37.43** | **+9.34** |
| Conv-DoRA (all blocks) | 29.02 | 32.64 | +3.62 |

**Cost of adapters in Stage 2/3: ~5.7 PQ lost.** Full backprop already has unlimited capacity; adapters add noise and corrupt pretrained features that propagate into the EMA teacher.
