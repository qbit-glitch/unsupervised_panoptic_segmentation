# 2026-04-01

## MMGD-Cut Ablation Complete (Rounds 1+2)

Built and evaluated **MMGD-Cut** (Multi-Modal Graph-Diffused K-way NCut) — a novel model combining multi-modal feature fusion + graph diffusion with Falcon NCut.

### Results
- **Best: 45.86% mIoU** (DINOv3+SD fusion, K=54, alpha=5.5, reg_lambda=0.7)
- +3.84 over Falcon SD-only baseline (42.02%)
- DINOv3 alone: 45.79% — SD-1.4 adds only +0.07 (too correlated, r=0.877)
- Graph diffusion (feature/affinity, 3/5 steps): zero effect on any config

### Critical Insight
The bottleneck is **feature complementarity**, not the algorithm. DINOv3 and SD-1.4 affinities are too correlated (r=0.877). SSD-1B is genuinely complementary (r=0.772 with DINOv3).
